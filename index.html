Qwit

Privacy policy, terms of use and support · last updated 15 November 2026

The short version

Qwit works on your iPhone without an account. Your quit data stays on your phone unless you choose to sign in with Apple to back it up. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we never sell data.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your quit plan, cravings, slips, milestones and badges are stored in the app on your device.
  • Your chat with Sam, the AI companion, is stored on your device only.
  • Deleting the app deletes this data, unless you backed it up.

What leaves your iPhone, and why

  • An anonymous app ID. On first launch the app gets a random ID from our backend (Supabase, servers in the EU, Ireland). It is not linked to your name or email. It lets the AI features count a fair monthly allowance.
  • AI coach and AI companion messages. When you write to the coach or to Sam, your message, the recent conversation and a few numbers (days smoke-free, today's cravings, your reasons for quitting) are sent through our backend to Google's Gemini API to write a reply. We do not store these conversations on our servers. Google processes them under its API terms. Don't share anything in these chats you would not want processed this way.
  • Backup (optional). If you sign in with Apple, your quit data (profile, sessions, cravings, slips, milestones, badges) is copied to our backend so it survives a new phone or reinstall. Apple may give us your name and an email address, which can be a private relay address.
  • Human buddy (optional, needs sign-in). If you look for a buddy, we store that you are waiting, your quit date, your pairing and the preset messages you exchange. Your buddy sees only those messages, never your name, email or data. Reports you make about a buddy are stored so we can review them.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, no analytics SDKs, no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No selling or sharing of your data for marketing.
  • No medical advice. Qwit is a support tool, not a medical device.

Your choices

  • Delete your account any time in Settings → Account → Delete account. This removes your backup, buddy data and account from our servers.
  • Without an account, deleting the app removes everything on your phone. The anonymous ID left behind holds no personal data.
  • To ask what we hold about you, or for a copy, email us (below). EU residents have the rights in the GDPR, including access, correction, deletion and complaint to a data protection authority.

Processors

  • Supabase (database, sign-in, server functions), EU region.
  • Google (Gemini API) for AI replies.
  • Apple (Sign in with Apple).

Children

Qwit is for adults who smoke. It is not directed at children under 16.

Terms of use

  • Qwit is provided as is, to help you quit smoking. It does not replace a doctor, pharmacist or stop-smoking service. If you feel unwell, contact a health professional or your local emergency number.
  • AI replies are generated by a computer. They can be wrong and are not medical advice. Sam is an AI, not a person.
  • Be kind to your buddy. We may end pairings and block accounts that are reported for abuse or spam.
  • Apple's standard licensed application end user licence agreement (EULA) also applies.

Changes

If what we collect changes, this page will be updated before the app update that changes it, and the App Store privacy label with it.
